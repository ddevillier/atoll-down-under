# Create, test, revise, keep, return

Scope: the creation editor, temporary test swim, My creations shelf, custom guide and rename dialog. The first kept life is a small grazer learning a refuge. Visitor mode is operate, with observation and a familiar fish as the reward.

Audience and job: a player imagining a fish, trying its movement and ecological choices, then keeping a life they can return to. Make the effect of diet, size, ability and budget readable before entry. Clearly distinguish invented game adaptations from real species.

Action sequence: name and shape a fish; Test swim; Change my fish to revise the retained draft; Keep and meet the reef; return through My creations. The shelf distinguishes Resume from New swim and offers Make a variation and Rename. A variation opens a draft based on the saved fish. The test toolbar explicitly states that nothing is saved and permits Reset test.

Direction: extend the incumbent field-guide paper dialogs over the painted reef. This is an ordinary extension and does not need a new visual world. The editor pairs the visible animal and appearance controls with living traits and their explanations. The collection pairs each familiar portrait with its habitat, life and remembered experiences. Existing typography, actions, focus and modal behavior remain the visual vocabulary.

Memorable moment: the named fish turns toward the pointer in the editor, then becomes a recognizable life on the shelf. For the small grazer, choosing shelter, finding a needed meal and returning home gives that life a simple ecological routine.

Content and proof: show diet and shelter fit in words, an ability explanation, remaining points, and the complete “Test swims never change saved fish.” sentence. The shelf gives the habitat, life number and available memories, or a short new-life invitation. Saving errors remain visible through existing feedback surfaces.

Constraints: preserve the two-column desktop editor and readable actions at 1280×720 and 1920×1080; keep portrait canvases contained; retain the established reef paintings and fonts. Do not let creation framing imply the invented fish is a real species. This brief does not expand the task into an identity redesign or unrelated product-context cleanup.

Evidence: all four `artifacts/creation-{editor,shelf}-{1280,1920}.png` captures; implementation in the three mapped files; bounded visual review `docs/CREATION-UI-REVIEW.md`, disposition ship. The complete test-swim sentence is readable at both reviewed sizes. Runtime verification remains separate from that screenshot verdict.

Unresolved visual decisions: none within the reviewed creation surfaces.
