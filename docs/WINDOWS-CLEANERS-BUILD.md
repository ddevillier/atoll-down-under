Extract the ZIP and launch `Atoll Down Under.exe`. Windows x64 only. Fullscreen starts automatically; F11 toggles it and Escape pauses.

Click a cleaner, choose **Visit cleaner**, or press **C**. Your fish approaches its station, waits for an existing client, and receives gradual parasite treatment. Steer away to cancel and retain any healing already received. Cleaners are safe helpers and cannot be eaten. Treatment leaves bite and poison damage for ordinary recovery. Try visits in both day and night reefs.
