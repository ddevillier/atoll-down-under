# Living Reef refinement

This Windows testing build improves the life and readability of the reef while preserving free exploration, all species journeys, created fish, saved dives and offline narration. Practice has low danger for shared family play. Predators remain part of the ecosystem.

## Swimming and interactions

Continuous turn centers remove the sideways jump when a fish changes facing. Propulsion follows actual displacement with distinct strokes, coasting and braking across 52 fish profiles. The painted fish retain their identity; four sharks also move their painted lower jaws during real feeding. Specialist ray, eel, cuttlefish and bottom-dweller rigs keep their existing motion, including the previous braking response.

Cleaner wrasses and coral shrimp use real approach, inspection and departure progress to move along a client. The client responds with local fin motion. Care removes parasites; it does not heal bite or poison damage. Displayed mouth and picking landmarks follow the rendered pose while simulation contact rules remain separate.

Brief visual marks indicate actual meals, cleaning, release, reunions, home returns and discoveries. Pause freezes them. Reduced motion holds decorative poses without suppressing simulation outcomes. Routine journey narration is announced accessibly without covering the water with callouts. Blocked actions and short predator warnings remain visible.

## Quiet screens

The title keeps its painted composition and compact File, Game and Help menus. Mode descriptions are short. Activity instructions, transcripts, natural history, score explanations and setting details open on request. New profiles start with control reminders off; existing explicit preferences are preserved. Field guide content, keyboard controls, storage errors and audio fallback remain available.

The October 10 follow-up shortens all floating interaction labels. Idle, working and hovered cleaners say “Cleaner”. Food hints and selected meals say “Food”. Activity places use names such as “Home”, “Patrol” and “Lookout”, with instructions kept in activity details. Hover names no longer append behavior commentary. Status tooltips use short labels; accessible descriptions remain available.

Shelter labels and rings appear only for fish smaller than 125, matching the existing hiding rule. This includes inspection, kept creations and temporary test swims. A marked home or refuge does not receive a duplicate generic shelter label. Larger animals can still mark a journey home or resting ledge; this is a return location, not a claim that they can hide there. The new browser matrix checks all 57 Practice species, both size classes in all nine habitats, and kept/test fish at sizes 124, 125 and 180 at both desktop widths.

The status icon lets mouse steering pass through to the reef. Expanded activity prose also lets pointers through, while its buttons and summary remain clickable. The HUD accommodates long diets at 1600 and 1024 pixels wide. The same review covers creation, journal, map, saves, settings, help, inspection and endings.

## Gentle ecology

Practice contact bites deal 18 damage, followed by four seconds of shared grace. Bite recovery begins after five seconds, at 1.2 health per second when nourishment allows it. Challenge and Tournament retain their prior 35 damage, 1.8 seconds of grace, ten-second delay and 0.4 recovery rate. Cleaning, hunger, poison, shelter and pursuit rules still apply.

A real NPC meal can invite up to two existing hungry schoolmates of the same species into a short feeding excursion. Followers seek real food and regroup; they receive no invented meals. Danger, cleaning, death, travel and loss of food interrupt the routine. The excursion expires after ten seconds and saves validate individual identities. NPC excursions do not advance the player's optional journey or award its discoveries.
