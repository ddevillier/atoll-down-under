## s30 — A coral housekeeper

### Evidence, identity and limits

The Australian museum-backed account identifies the three-barred white-tailed *D. aruanus*, aggregating at branching corals and feeding on zooplankton. Use a Pacific/Great Barrier Reef case study. The Indian Ocean sibling was resurrected as *D. abudafur* by Borsa and colleagues in **2014**; its distribution includes the Red Sea. This is a substantive boundary for older studies using the name *D. aruanus*, not merely a renamed source citation. The museum account's reproductive paragraph contains genus-level generalizations, insufficient by themselves for a species-specific sex-change story. [Fishes of Australia](https://fishesofaustralia.net.au/home/species/347), [Borsa et al. 2014, primary taxonomic study](https://comptes-rendus.academie-sciences.fr/biologies/articles/10.1016/j.crvi.2014.09.001/).

A controlled experiment explicitly used *D. aruanus* with *Pocillopora damicornis*. Occupied corals retained markedly less deposited sediment than fish-free controls. The authors describe fish picking up sediment with their mouths and blowing particles away, alongside water stirring. This supports a physical home-care interaction. It does not establish fish altruistically diagnosing coral illness, restoring bleached coral instantly, or farming algae. [Chase et al. 2020, article and institutional full text](https://researchonline.jcu.edu.au/63854/) / [published PDF](https://researchonline.jcu.edu.au/63854/1/Chase%20et%20al.%202020%20Roy%20Soc%20Open%20Sci%20-%20Fishes%20moderate%20sediment%20impacts.pdf).

**New decision:** choose where and from which direction to clear a lightly dusted colony so particles leave a sheltered pocket. This is spatial manipulation with a changed coral surface, rather than feeding and returning.

### Implementation card — The coral housekeeper

- **Four beats:** (1) Swim to a rendered branching colony and inspect the dust lodged between branches; choose one of two reachable pockets. (2) Click the dusty pocket to approach; only at mouth contact does one gentle clearing action start. (3) Move around the colony and choose an outward approach to the remaining pocket; a short fin/blowing effect moves particles clear. Each pocket requires a deliberate action, not repeated clicks or a quota. (4) Deliberately settle beside the cleared passage and inspect the existing branch detail. A real resident may resume using it; that is a bonus, never a random mandatory gate.
- **World/NPC consequence:** persistent local sediment mask and navigable-looking opening; an eligible existing humbug can use normal refuge steering to occupy an available branch space. No new NPC is spawned for the scene. Clear masks should reveal polyp artwork, not imply immediate tissue recovery or new coral growth.
- **Source hooks to reuse:** `homeJourneySites`/refuge IDs and proximity conventions; `reef-stories.js` module interface (`initStory`, `storyAction`, `tickStory`, `storyNPC`, `storyView`, `validStory`); normal steering; `feeding-contact.js` mouth geometry as an architectural pattern without calling nutrition or consuming coral; `reef-memories.js` care memory; discovery event and `STORY_IDS`/journal persistence.
- **New state/art/sound:** the draft now provides typed `COLONY` geometry in `coral-housekeeper.js`: reef 4, `pocillopora-cathedral-1`, branch endpoints, two targets and an inspection berth. Preserve `sim.reefStories.housekeeper` with validated site/reef, two binary masks, pocket selection, direction, intent/phase and bounded clearing/plume clocks. The separate renderer supplies dust and revealed branch surfaces; wire its contact-pose override to the authoritative mouth. Optional resident identity is needed only if resident flavor is subsequently added. Reuse subdued water effects; no voice dependency.
- **Input/physics:** current mouse movement calls `sim.point`, while click calls `sim.eatAt`. Connect the draft `storyClick`, `storyPointer` and `storyPlayer` hooks before ordinary eating/movement. A click selects travel; it never clears remotely. E/context supplies the complete keyboard sequence. An inward choice produces a visible plume that settles into shelter; only an outward mouth-contact action changes the mask. This direction puzzle is **authored interpretation**, not evidence that wild fish solve a two-choice exit puzzle. Pointer movement cancels immediately without erasing cleared pockets.
- **Save/pause/offscreen:** validate bounded masks and site IDs, reject impossible complete state, default missing story state for older saves. Pause freezes particles and progress. Inactive/offscreen reef gets no new dust or damage; return preserves work. Missing or reincarnated residents release associations. Story completion adds a unique durable discovery; replay does not duplicate it or consume the normal home journey badge.
- **Acceptance:** start Practice as s30 through real UI, begin story, click a far pocket and verify no change before approach/contact; swim to its other side and visibly change the particle exit direction; clear the accessible pockets without eating a meal; pause mid-action and verify freeze; save/reload and return from another reef with masks preserved; verify one journal discovery across replay. A missing nearby NPC must not block the finish.
- **Effort/priority:** **M / P0**. Main cost is honest coral geometry and interactive dust targets, not a new food type. Site-directed clearing choice is an authored simplification; do not label exact particle steering as measured fish cognition.

### Later card — A home that breathes at night (identity gate)

Goldshmid and colleagues observed continuous nighttime fin movements in three coral-sheltering fishes, including fish then called *D. aruanus*. **The full primary paper places the study in Eilat, Red Sea.** In light of the later split, those humbugs are likely modern *D. abudafur*; that is a locality-based inference, not a re-examination of vouchers. Both the gypsum flow experiment and the laboratory oxygen manipulations specifically used ***D. marginatus***. Neither their numerical effect sizes nor the old humbug name establishes an exact-species Pacific *D. aruanus* effect. Retain the attractive design below, but require independent Pacific evidence or an explicitly named *D. abudafur* case study before attaching it to s30. [Goldshmid et al. 2004, full primary paper, methods pp. 1833–1834](https://aslopubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/pdf/10.4319/lo.2004.49.5.1832), [Borsa et al. 2014](https://comptes-rendus.academie-sciences.fr/biologies/articles/10.1016/j.crvi.2014.09.001/).

- **Four beats:** choose a free branch pocket at dusk; move the mouse among nearby rest positions to notice which interior spaces receive flow; settle and watch automatic sleep-swimming start; inspect fine suspended particles moving through previously still spaces. Existing humbugs independently settle into other available spaces, when present. The decision is where to rest, not a rhythm/fanning minigame: sleep behavior should remain automatic.
- **Consequence:** a readable flow field and quietly moving resident fins make the coral appear alive after dark. No oxygen deadline, danger meter, or requirement to stay awake clicking.
- **Hooks/new work:** reuse the colony descriptor above, fin motion, refuge separation, story rendering, care memory and journal; add rest-slot/flow state and sleep fin pose. **Important blocker:** `Simulation.start` fixes period from species activity and this day-active fish starts by day; no ordinary dusk transition was found. A transparent optional in-world dusk transition with preserved populations is new infrastructure, not a hidden `period='night'` assignment or respawn.
- **Persistence/acceptance:** freeze rest/flow progression when paused or offscreen; save slot and same resident identities. Test reachable dusk choice through actual controls, no teleport, physically settle before flow starts, mouse movement wakes/cancels rest, pause freezes, reload preserves discovery. An empty colony still permits player-only demonstration. **Hold / L** until the exact identity and explicit dusk gates pass; **M** if valid identity evidence and safe day/night infrastructure already exist. A continuously moving fin animation alone cannot earn the discovery.

**Deferred lead:** a 2022 observational article reports cleaning by *D. aruanus*. Publisher and university records were opened, but full behavioral details were subscription-restricted. Do not build a second Reef Clinic from its title alone. [Parmentier et al.](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s12526-022-01275-3), [University of Liège record](https://orbi.uliege.be/handle/2268/293633).

## s1 — A little room beneath a rock

### Evidence and identity

The museum account supports the neon-blue/yellow-tail identity and warns implicitly against confusing it with *P. pavo* through its comparison. [Fishes of Australia](https://fishesofaustralia.net.au/home/species/2341).

Jan’s exact-species field study describes *P. coelestis* as a free-ranging demersal spawner, generally without permanent substrate occupancy, excavating small burrows/depressions beneath boulders. Spawning-site competition also occurs; omit forced evictions from this peaceful design. The study is northern Taiwan, not proof that every Australian habitat has identical nesting geology. [Jan 1997, primary PDF](https://zoolstud.sinica.edu.tw/Journals/36.1/26.pdf). The now-readable earlier primary study shows nest excavation beside a rock (Fig. 8) and describes males leading females down to prepared burrows, with eggs deposited mainly on nest roofs. Nest preparation can therefore have an explicitly chosen adult-male role; courtship/eggs remain outside this small card. [Jan & Ormond 1992, primary paper, p. 240](https://zoolstud.sinica.edu.tw/Journals/31.4/231.pdf).

**Identity gate:** this is the *P. coelestis* studied in Taiwan, consistent with the roster's scientific identity. A morphologically similar Micronesian lineage was described as *P. micronesicus*; do not transfer every historic aquarium or regional “neon damsel” account without checking locality. [Liu, Ho & Dai 2013, primary taxonomic paper](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/1810-522X-52-6).

**Verdict:** the current coral refuge is a game home anchor, not evidence for an algal farm or permanent farming territory. Add building behavior if desired; do not rename the existing algae/plankton meal loop as farming.

### Implementation card — A room beneath the rock

- **Four beats:** inspect two nearby boulder undersides; choose the shaded, accessible pocket; click loose substrate to approach and clear an entrance, then choose a side approach to widen its obstructed lip; swim physically into the new recess and turn to look out. A visible small chamber and nest-preparation discovery are the finish. No brood or partner is created merely because the player clicked.
- **Decisions/consequence:** shape the usable opening rather than collect meals. A shallow open depression and under-rock pocket are distinct visuals; feedback explains why the latter resembles the observed nesting site. The completed pocket remains visible and available as the player’s rest anchor. If a real nearby s1 investigates, it swims there through existing steering; this is optional authored social flavor, not evidence that inspection guarantees mating.
- **Hooks:** reuse sand target/contact concepts from `sand-search.js`, home site selection, story action/view/validation contract, `rememberExperience` and discovery journal. Do not call `uncoverSand` unchanged: this action excavates shelter, not food, and must award no calories.
- **New state/art/sound:** boulder-pocket stable ID, bounded entrance-clearance state, chosen approach side, progress/completion; shallow substrate plume, revealed dark recess, local mouth/fin action. Use existing water/soft scrape assets only if fitting.
- **Controls and constraints:** opt into the adult nesting-male role before construction; do not infer sex from the generic player sprite. Mouse selects physical work target; E works only nearby; movement interrupts without loss. Collision/contact and reachable entrance geometry, not click count, determine clearance. Persist edited pocket and badge; pause/offscreen do not add work or collapse the nest; no fabricated clutch, partner or teleport. Label this as nest preparation; actual egg care is a separate lifecycle feature.
- **Acceptance:** actual Practice UI s1; choose either site and receive informative feedback; a far click produces travel only; completed contact changes geometry, not energy; enter newly cleared recess by swimming; save/reload restores geometry and badge once. Verify the pre-existing `damsel-territory` journey still works separately. **M / P1.**

## s2 — Yellow on the outer edge

### Evidence, identity and limits

The repository’s legacy common name says Orange coral goby, but its scientific identity is the **yellow coralgoby**, *G. okinawae*. It occupies outer edges of sheltered tabular *Acropora*, consumes plankton and some coral tissue, and can move between colonies. Its toxic skin secretions are a passive defense; bright appearance and exposed perching conflict with presenting it solely as a camouflage specialist. [Fishes of Australia](https://fishesofaustralia.net.au/home/species/2231).

Cole and Hoese’s exact-species gonad/experimental study supports capacity for bidirectional sexual function in adult hermaphrodites, while some individuals function solely as females. That is more nuanced than ‘any two gobies always change sex and form a pair.’ Only the published abstract was accessible. [Cole & Hoese 2001](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1023/A:1011032228716).

An additional primary study specifically describes *G. okinawae* spending much of its time on top of colonies and often moving among them, rather than remaining deep inside one host. It also found limited sustained air respiration in this species compared with several more sedentary coralgobies. This strengthens **visible perching and voluntary host transfer**, not an air-breathing endurance challenge. [Nilsson et al. 2007, primary full text, habitat Table 1 and results](https://jean-paulhobbs.weebly.com/uploads/2/8/0/9/28093327/nilsson_et_al_2007a_hypoxia_tolerence_airbreathing_coral_reefs.pdf).

**Verdict:** improve its current home interaction first. The famous chemical-alarm/seaweed-removal experiment used ***Gobiodon histrio*** and ***Paragobiodon echinocephalus*** with ***Acropora nasuta***, not *G. okinawae*. Do not transplant that rescue loop into this roster identity. [Dixson & Hay 2012, primary study abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23139333/). Do not add skin-toxin shooting, unconditional predator immunity, an air-survival trial, or an instant partner/sex-change button. A dedicated coral-rescue story lacks exact-species support; the useful spatial home enhancement below is better grounded.

### Succinct enhancement plan — The outer branch

**S–M / P2:** extend existing `coral-home` rather than add another feeding quota. Inspect a visible outer perch and a sheltered comparison perch in a typed tabular *Acropora* colony; physically settle at the chosen edge and see the different occlusion and posture. Then choose to keep this familiar home or swim across to a second free colony, where the selected home anchor visibly changes. Exposed perching and frequent colony movement are observations; the two-perch comparison and deliberate “keep or move” sequence are authored interpretation. A plankton bite is optional ordinary feeding, with actual mouth contact. Preserve the existing homecoming badge unless the spatial sequence merits its own distinct discovery.

Reuse `shelter.js`, s2-specific adventure branches and home anchors. Share the **typed-site pattern**, not Housekeeper's *Pocillopora* colony: this fish requires compatible *Acropora* artwork and host evidence. Store a bounded reef/site/perch reference and the chosen home; render real underside occlusion and pelvic support at the perch, rather than a hovering fish hidden by a proximity flag. Avoid silently changing the plankton-only diet. Pause/offscreen preserve home/perch state. Acceptance: E/mouse physically reach both perches; far clicks never settle remotely; a voluntary crossing updates only the chosen host and survives reload; occupied hosts stay occupied; cancellation resumes ordinary swimming; old saves and the existing homecoming badge still work. No prey, predator or second adult is needed to finish. Reproduction remains a separately gated lifecycle feature.

## Cross-cutting implementation guardrails

All proposals are optional Practice stories/enhancements. No grind, daily upkeep, countdown, egg loss or offscreen death. Discovery badges should persist through `DISCOVERIES`, journal validation/merge and export/import, while per-dive world changes belong in versioned story state. `freshStories`/`validStories` currently whitelist five modules; adding a story requires updating that whitelist and backward-compatible save validation, not attaching unvalidated properties. Keep real fish identity as reef + ID + incarnation; recycled population IDs cannot inherit relationships. Existing NPC safety, food and cleaner priorities outrank authored social behavior. Reduced motion must retain readable dust/flow state without decorative movement being the only evidence of success.
