# Sand detectives: evidence and next-adventure decisions

Reviewed 10 October 2026 against current source. Scope: **s9, s23, s50, s55**. Research only; no runtime changes, new assets, population edits or playtest claims. P1 = accuracy/prerequisite work; P2 = bounded prototype after the six core adventures; Hold = missing identity or evidence. S/M/L describe relative implementation scope.

A changed menu or another two-meal quota is enrichment, not a distinct adventure. Each proposed new card below includes a choice with a physical consequence and its own completion receipt. All route shapes, distances, timings and local response rules are authored interpretations.

## s9 — Retain Whisker Detective; resolve identity before collaboration

**Observed / source.** The [Waikīkī Aquarium family account](https://www.waikikiaquarium.org/experience/animal-guide/fishes/goatfishes/goatfishes/) supports sensory barbels and substrate searching across goatfishes, while noting varied diets and activity periods. It cannot identify this yellow sprite as Parupeneus cyclostomus. [Strübin, Steinegger & Bshary 2011, primary abstract](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/j.1439-0310.2011.01966.x) reports P. cyclostomus adults living alone or in stable, similarly sized groups: solitary searching on sand versus collaborative mobile-prey hunts around corals. Partners took different routes around coral to obstruct escape. This is a verified candidate behavior for that species, not a family-wide trait or evidence of fairness.

**Current feature.** `whisker-detective.js` already binds at most two actual buried meals in reefs 6/7, provides a local barbel cue, requires anatomical contact probing, reserves food for an explicit eat/leave decision, and selects an existing compatible neighbor by ID/incarnation. The neighbor swims to the real food and takes an actual bite; nearby witnessing is required. `detective-substrate.js`, `sand-search.js`, `feeding-contact.js` and `whisker-detective-render.js` supply contact and presentation. The current `natural-history.js` limit now explains this simplified search/sharing honestly; the earlier wording correction is already complete. Leaving a snack is a game invitation, not demonstrated deliberate generosity.

**Conditional card — Around the Other Side (M–L / Hold).** Only after a documented species/art decision establishing P. cyclostomus: observe an eligible existing conspecific pursuing an existing small prey toward a real coral obstacle → choose direct pursuit or a separate route → physically occupy a reachable escape lane while the other fish takes its own route → observe the prey's resulting direction, then disengage. The distinction is complementary space use, not another food-sharing badge. Do not guarantee a catch or reward a particular role.

**Files / art / state.** Add a selected story through `reef-story-data.js` / `reef-stories.js`; use `ecology.js`, ordinary pursuit/contact, and `geometry.js`. Author one obstacle with two connected water routes and actual openings; show diverging trajectories and barbel contact if prey enters a crevice. Bound one partner and one prey as `{reef,id,incarnation,species}`, stage, route choice and encounter receipt. No artificial prey, partner teleport, broken pair, appetite refill or population increase. Admission must find suitable live actors and honor committed meals/care/danger; otherwise do not offer Begin.

**Acceptance.** A remote “block” button cannot redirect prey. The blocking body must reach the correct lane while the partner takes the other route; absent/replaced actors release ownership. Cancel restores free movement immediately. Pause/offscreen freeze; restored bindings revalidate. Preserve Detective selection, saves, discovered food and witness behavior. **Verdict: keep current play; identity-gated queue only.**

## s23 — Five Fins, One Box

**Observed / source.** The museum's [L. cornuta account](https://fishesofaustralia.net.au/home/species/837) supports sand-directed water jets. More distinctive evidence comes from [Van Gorp et al. 2022, author-hosted primary paper](https://medialibrary.uantwerpen.be/files/13304/30546f79-1d6f-431b-a5cb-c03d8e40cef8.pdf), which explicitly includes L. cornuta museum specimen LACM 38229 in five-species computational comparisons. Its rigid carapace cannot perform ordinary body undulation. Keels increased resistance to rolling; they did **not** establish passive stabilization of yaw/pitch or lower drag. These are models of anatomical forces, not measured wild route choices or an exact maneuvering algorithm.

**Current feature.** `animal-profiles.js` already gives a rigid, fin-driven appearance and solitary sand routine. `adventures.js` E within 110 pixels calls `uncoverSand` immediately, then asks for two uncovered meals; `routineStep` also reveals NPC sand food. Adding another reveal quota would repeat current play.

**Choice → consequence.** Inspect a broad bend and a shorter offset opening → choose an approach side and align the rigid body → use ordinary pointer steering to ease through, with visible pectoral/dorsal/anal/caudal fin work → settle on the far side and reverse out or take the broader route home. A wrong approach meets a readable clearance boundary; turning or retreat changes what is passable. The body stays rigid while its fins articulate. Completion requires an actual turn/traversal and voluntary exit, with no meal or toxin use. Routes and clearance puzzle are authored; do not label the body automatically self-steering.

**Files / art / state.** Add a bounded story around `steering.js`, `geometry.js`, `animal-profiles.js`, `animal-rigs.js`, `rigid-motion.js`, `fin-motion.js`, renderer dispatch and story registry. Author two short water corridors from existing reef art, with an authoritative horn-inclusive collision hull, entrance axes and one exit. Verify rig landmarks against the actual painting. Persist one route/site key, stage, bounded turn/travel progress and actor identity. No global current simulation is needed; if a local current is added later, its visible vector must affect movement and be explicitly described as authored.

**Acceptance / gate.** A diagonal body or projecting horn cannot pass because its center is near a marker. Movement and fin strokes must agree in motion recordings; no sprite bending to fake clearance. Cancellation permits retreat without damage or a locked pose. Save during turning/entry/exit revalidates actual body placement; pause and offscreen cannot finish. Reduced motion preserves orientation/clearance cues. **M / P2**, conditional on visible fin control and real hull fit; otherwise retain the existing routine.

**Other research, deliberately excluded.** [Parmentier et al. 2020/2021, primary methods p.2](https://orbi.uliege.be/bitstream/2268/252194/1/120_Lactoria_2020.pdf) recorded hums/clicks from fish held near a hydrophone. [The 2025 acoustic-evolution paper](https://orbi.uliege.be/bitstream/2268/337784/1/160_boxfish_2025_auteur_version.pdf), publicized in February 2026, compares sonic anatomy and reuses L. cornuta data; it does not establish a wild greeting, courtship conversation or friend-calling mechanic. Do not transfer spawning sounds from L. fornasini or Ostracion meleagris. A mouth-origin aimed jet is a worthwhile M/P2 refinement to the current sand routine, using `mouthPoint` / `uncoverSand`, but is not the ranked new adventure.

## s50 — Correct the schedule; separate daytime resting play

**Observed / source.** [Museums Victoria / Fishes of Australia](https://fishesofaustralia.net.au/home/species/2222) documents daytime aggregations and nocturnal benthic feeding in G. aureolineatus. This supports distinct grouping and feeding contexts; it does not prove social rank, chosen lookout roles or a particular shoal response algorithm. Keep this exact taxon; studies of Lethrinus emperors do not establish its behavior.

**Current feature and correction.** `data.js` sets `active:'day'` with fish/crab/snail and `crush`. `foraging-journeys.js` requires crab handling followed by a fish hunt; `natural-history.js` explicitly admits the schedule mismatch. `animal-profiles.js` labels it a loose bottom forager, not a dedicated resting shoal. First audit `communities.js` and `simulation.js` night initialization, then put fresh feeding dives in the documented nighttime context. Preserve old daytime saves and their animals/period; explain the legacy scene or offer a separate fresh night dive. This **S–M/P1 accuracy correction** does not by itself deliver a new adventure. A crab-or-snail branch can improve the existing feeding trip but still remains feeding enrichment.

**New card — Room in the Resting Shoal (M / P2).** Explicitly choose a daytime observation dive → inspect an existing local aggregation → choose an edge position or the available gap between neighbors → swim into it while keeping a body-sized separation → observe nearby fish adjust locally, then leave the gap and see their spacing relax. The payoff is a reversible configuration of living fish, not calories, a calm timer or a guaranteed protection bonus. Spacing responses are authored; describe joining a resting group, not decoded fish approval.

**Files / art / state.** `ecology.js`, `communities.js`, story registry, `geometry.js` and renderer need a bounded daytime resting-group override for existing eligible conspecifics. Persist no more than three identity-safe actors, one local anchor, chosen position, stage and witnessed spacing receipt. Art uses reduced cruising amplitude, actual headings and small gap cues. Admission must secure those fish inside the existing initial adult budget without teleporting adults, stealing commitments or resetting appetite. The large wild aggregation size is not a population target.

**Acceptance / gate.** Choosing “middle” remotely cannot spread the group: actual body distance drives response. Both edge and gap choices must produce different readable local spacing; departure restores normal ownership. No conspecific group means this daytime card is unavailable, with a clear fresh-scene alternative. Freeze pause/offscreen, preserve old periods, cancel safely, and resume the same identities. No compulsory sunset wait, predator confrontation, generic fear pulse, fish kill or live day/night rewrite.

## s55 — Familiar Water

**Observed / source.** [The museum account](https://fishesofaustralia.net.au/home/species/4279) establishes P. georgianus, its reef/sand schooling and southern Australasian range; the game's tropical layout is fictional context. [Fowler, Chick & Stewart 2018, exact-species primary study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-018-34922-6) found many adults recaptured near release locations, with occasional much longer movements. Tag/recapture measures endpoints; it does not establish navigation cues, route memory, deliberate exploration or every individual's fidelity. Its discussion distinguishes P. dentex studies. Do not import Azores P. dentex homing, migration distances or ram-filtering claims.

**Current feature.** `patrol-journeys.js` forces a 360-pixel midwater survey, shrimp then fish, and a three-second calm return to a static starting coordinate. `animal-profiles.js` already labels it a schooling cruiser. `school-activity.js` feeding waves are plankton-specific; reusing them would require unsupported diet changes.

**Choice → physical consequence.** Inspect two recognizable existing reef/sand landmarks → choose a short local circuit or an excursion across one reef boundary → physically depart by the selected route → return through the matching entrance and inspect the original landmark. The local choice keeps the original surroundings continuously available; the excursion shows a different existing community before the return. A compact route trace records the choice, not a food counter. Discovery requires actual departure, traversal and landmark inspection, not proximity or waiting. This is player-directed navigation inspired by variable movement extent, not a claim of experimentally demonstrated landmark learning.

**Files / art / state.** Make it a separately selected story using `reef-story-data.js`, `reef-stories.js`, map/travel in `simulation.js`, `REEFS` and `geometry.js`; keep the old patrol available. Author two stable landmark IDs using existing backgrounds, an exit/return entrance and physical inspection range. Persist origin reef/site, one route enum, stage and bounded visited/departed/returned receipts. An optional existing trevally group can cruise independently as scenery; it must never gate completion or be spawned for a reunion. No new temperate biome, juvenile art, compulsory appetite change or prey is needed.

**Acceptance / gate.** A map click selects a route but cannot count travel. Local and cross-reef choices must yield different visits and both remain completable without a meal/neighbor. Inspection requires actual return; browser blur, pause or offscreen elapsed time cannot fabricate it. Saved stages resume without resetting the origin; cancellation preserves ordinary navigation. **M/P2**, only if route choice changes the scene experienced and the final interaction tests recognition of the authored landmark; reject a reskinned static patrol return.

## Shared delivery boundaries

These are proposals, not implemented discoveries. Preserve all 64 roster identities and existing journeys; no resident becomes playable. New story saves need bounded enums/arrays, actual actor/target identities, strict validation and compatibility with earlier journals. Freeze paused/offscreen clocks, revalidate travel/death/incarnation changes, and allow immediate cancellation. Held physical interactions need the core local peaceful-encounter policy and need-drain suspension without nutrition refills or permanent/global immunity. Verify through normal pointer/keyboard motion, pause/save/import and live-world consequences before claiming a feature shipped. No runtime files, narration, assets or builds were changed for this research pass.
