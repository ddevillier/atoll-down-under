# Creation component art

Generated with the built-in image tool on September 10, 2026. The initial rendered prototype exposed flat body material and angular fins; these independent painted components replace that material. Local background removal is authorized by the user. No original Odell artwork is used.

## Body-core prompt

Create a production sprite sheet for an original high-resolution reef creature creation game. Genuinely transparent RGBA background. THREE isolated fish BODY CORES arranged in THREE evenly spaced horizontal rows, no labels or divider lines. Each faces RIGHT with head right and a very short narrow caudal peduncle at left; completely omit every fin and the tail fin because those are separately rigged components. Top row: deep oval disk-shaped reef fish body with gently pointed small mouth. Middle row: slender elongated torpedo-shaped reef fish body with small pointed head. Bottom row: sturdy grouper-like fish body with broad blunt head and large mouth. Each has one realistic dark eye, clear gill cover, tiny natural scales, lustrous silvery blue-gray skin with ivory belly, dark slate upper back and subtle highlights. NO colored markings, no spots, no stripes. Natural-history field-guide painting, volumetric anatomical shading, refined brush texture, realistic living fish, not cartoon, not flat vector, not a diagram. Clean unbroken opaque body silhouette, no internal transparent patches. Three complete separate cores each taking about 80% image width and 23% image height, generous transparent space between rows and margins. No water, scenery, ground, shadow, text, border or checkerboard. They are healthy fantasy game asset components, not dismembered animals. Landscape high resolution.

Consistent silvery warm ivory and muted blue-grey translucent membrane, visible delicate natural fin rays, refined natural-history painting, realistic soft organic curves, subtle volumetric lighting. ROW 1 four TAIL FINS in side view, narrow attachment root at RIGHT middle and broad trailing edge LEFT: (1) rounded fan with smoothly curved broad outer edge, (2) forked with two pointed lobes and a V notch, (3) lyretail with long graceful upper and lower tips curling outward and concave middle, (4) tapered tail ending in one point left. No water, scenery, shadows, text, borders or checkerboard. Soft organic anatomy, never polygonal origami or vector shapes. Landscape high resolution.

Sources are retained under the Codex generated-images folder. Final runtime components and their bounds are recorded after extraction; generated backgrounds are not intended for the game.

## Head-atlas prompt and final assets

Four separate right-facing fish heads in a 2×2 atlas: small mouth, broad grouper jaws, parrotfish beak, and long butterflyfish snout. Each has a broad attachment root behind the gills at left, realistic scales and gill plates, natural-history painted silvery blue-gray skin, and no fins or body. Transparent background requested. This paragraph summarizes the generation request; the model returned a dark background, which was removed locally.

Source images in the thread's Codex generated-images directory: bodies `exec-28a1b069-be5f-44fb-a002-d3a5b84a9b7e.png`, fins `exec-52e98741-5b9c-4d17-be6c-29067d07b3a3.png`, heads `exec-66906f99-45df-4b44-9c38-384a51147874.png`.

`scripts/prepare-creation-art.py` extracts the authored cells and uses local rembg/isnet matting. It keeps the main component, repairs internal alpha holes in opaque bodies/heads, trims distant haze, and adds transparent padding. Runtime uses the 19 PNGs in `assets/creatures/creation-v1/`. Extraction bounds are in `artifacts/anatomy-art-extraction.json`; source sheets and matting dependencies are excluded from the Windows app.
