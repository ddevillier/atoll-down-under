## Watching and short approaches checkpoint

The two loose-group browsers, trumpetfish and flashlight fish remain, alongside seven resident observation journeys, narration and the verified combined Windows release.

Players use E at an existing ledge, reef lookout or bottom-route watching place. Settling takes the species-specific interval. While watching, mouse movement aims the fish at actual nearby suitable food without steering away. A continuous brief look identifies a meal; clicking it starts an ordinary anatomical feeding approach. Journey credit requires that same target and incarnation, successful consumption and a bounded trip from the watching place. There is no countdown race or invented prey. E or an empty-water click releases watching; ordinary movement cancels a feeding approach. Travel, cleaning, defense and target loss safely release the interaction. Watching itself grants no additional concealment.

Most species return to their chosen place after feeding. Golden snappers return between a watched shrimp meal and a watched fish meal. Checkerboard hawkfish instead choose and settle at a different lookout. Finished activities release mouse-look mode and can be repeated while retaining journal recognition. Missing nearby opportunities are reported rather than manufactured. Watching/return state and gaze references are validated and round-trip through paused portable saves.
