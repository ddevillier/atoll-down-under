# Species lifestyles: implementation shortlist

Research refresh: 10 October 2026. Twenty researchers reviewed the complete 64-creature roster: 57 Practice-playable creatures and seven residents. This document selects work from the [complete research index](species-adventure-research/README.md); the twenty reports retain sources, alternative cards, limitations and explicit defer decisions for every creature.

## Direction

Give each adventure a different verb: tend, watch, carry, walk, fit, notice, navigate, investigate, share space, or approach from a useful angle. Feeding can support a story, but another eat-and-return quota is insufficient. Preserve peaceful, mysterious exploration, responsive animals, optional discoveries and earned badges. The educational explanation follows the interaction; sound and narration remain optional.

These are biologically inspired game designs in a fictional mixed-species reef. A paper establishes a behavior in its particular animals and setting; our choices, short timescales, controls and reward choreography are authored. Family/genus labels need named case studies before species-specific claims. No original-game comparison was used.

## Strong ideas with an explicit prerequisite

- **Anemonefish / host — A Mouthful for the Host:** named *Amphiprion clarkii* / *Entacmaea quadricolor* case study. Choose a finite morsel, carry it to a tentacle socket and watch inward transfer. Requires matching fish/host art and a non-food carry target; host ingestion is the payoff, not instant growth or player calories. Recent field experiments support provisioning, not universal altruism. [02](species-adventure-research/02-anemone-partners.md).
- **Humphead wrasse — A Cave with Room:** body clearance and existing arrivals at a sleeping cave. Requires an explicit dusk/rest scene and no forced clock wait. Distinguish passage geometry from the triggerfish's spine lock. [05](species-adventure-research/05-specialist-mouths.md).
- **Banggai cardinalfish — Before the Nursery:** opt into courtship and egg handoff, then enter the existing brood adventure. Real compatible partners, explicit adult role and clutch state are prerequisites; retain the completed nursery. **Yellow seahorse:** a resident pair's rise/return needs reviewed second-resident allocation and holdfast release. [08](species-adventure-research/08-parenting.md).
- **Silver sprat / crocodile needlefish:** substrate selection and attached eggs need explicit reproductive roles and attachment artwork. No invented guarding or automatic adult population growth. [16](species-adventure-research/16-schools-light.md), [11](species-adventure-research/11-pelagic-hunters.md).
- **Nudibranch / snail / sea star / urchin:** autonomous sensory turning, finite grazing tracks or planted tube-foot motion can make rewarding observations. Choose a vantage or environmental cue; the resident makes the movement. Named representatives are needed for species-specific diet/defense claims. Inspection markers must not steer the resident. [20](species-adventure-research/20-nudibranch.md), [19](species-adventure-research/19-bottom-residents.md).
- **Banded coral shrimp:** recognised-mate nook choices need a genuine second shrimp within the population budget. Environmental parasite-stage picking needs actual typed stage quantities and qualified laboratory framing first; current adults' care does not establish that substrate ecology. Do not add decorative egg clusters and claim prevented infections. [18](species-adventure-research/18-cleaners.md).

Small accuracy work belongs alongside these additions: yellowspot emperor currently starts in daytime although its benthic feeding is primarily nocturnal; preserve old-save periods while correcting new starts and offering a separately labelled daytime shoal view. Filefish odor observers in the primary methods are identified only as *Cephalopholis* spp.; our coral grouper is an authored genus-level proxy unless separately verified. Whitemouth moray is not the documented partner of our *Variola louti* in the cooperative-hunting observations.

Additional worthwhile cards—rainbow-runner scraping, blacktip familiar associations, electric-ray prey-directed pulses, real cryptofauna search, context-linked coral-trout patterns and group observation—remain in their roster reports. Their uncertain functions and missing-participant requirements are recorded there. Current-richness improvements such as bannerfish leadership or coral-goby perches should extend their existing journeys rather than manufacture another badge.

## Staged delivery contract

1. **Completed: shared integration and admission.** Story selection, movement/click/context/actor-aware meal hooks, painted loaders/layers, local safety and strict restore are connected. Initial rabbitfish/cohort availability, solid-outcrop placement and frogfish support geometry were corrected. Existing saves retain actor counts, bonds and current physiology; missing participants receive honest Retry or preserve-save/Fresh adventure dive guidance. The implementation record defines identity, pose validation, saved pause intent and cleanup contracts.
2. **Completed: actual-control acceptance for the seven additions.** Independent reviews drove ordinary pointer/E/buttons through both choices and viewport sizes, captured physical contact and changing motion, and verified cancellation, durable saves and single discoveries. Separate detached-save fixtures cover malformed/stale/missing/busy states and offscreen invariants. Local predator checks preserve outside behavior and safe release. Browser pause/handler evidence is distinguished from native OS background verification; final native/package and broader regression results remain in the release receipt. This acceptance establishes playable consequences, not family-tested fun.
3. **Build one selected new loop end to end.** Shared shark shelter is the first new behavioral category. Then add the two distinct mouth approaches and gated scent mystery. Reuse only helpers exposed by working cases; do not build a general ecosystem/quest framework in advance.
4. **Add explanation after the consequence.** One brief sourced reflection, optional offline voice, visible muted equivalents and a persistent journal discovery. Keep movement and choices readable at widescreen and smaller windows, fullscreen and reduced motion. Narration never becomes a completion gate.
5. **Verify and package.** Run relevant tests, existing-story regressions, real-input browser acceptance and packaged Windows checks. Profile real animation/school/particle costs before reducing detail. Commit/push and park a tested build only after the selected scope meets these gates. Adult/child playtesting remains the evidence for fun; automated completion is not a substitute.

Every new story stores bounded enums, stable site IDs and exact actor references `(reef,id,incarnation,species)`. Renderers cannot award progress. Remote clicks select travel, not completed contact. Pause/offscreen creates no work or punishment. Completed world changes and journal entries survive return; cancelled interactions immediately return control.
