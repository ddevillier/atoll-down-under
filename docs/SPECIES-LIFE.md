# Ways of living

Practice has 57 choices and 19 optional species adventures. Choose **Try adventure** for a little guidance, or **Enter the reef** to explore freely. Discoveries still count when the adventure card is hidden. The family journal keeps discoveries, badges and care stamps across dives.

| Species | Something to try |
| --- | --- |
| Cleaner wrasse, banded coral shrimp | Click a fish that needs cleaning. Help it remove parasites, then find another kind of client. Helping provides nourishment. Swim away to stop a visit. Cleaning does not heal bites or poison. |
| Palette surgeonfish, parrotfish | Graze algae, feed at another patch, then revisit the first patch as it recovers. Nearby grazers also make choices about available food. |
| Foxface rabbitfish, orange-spotted filefish, pencilled rabbitfish | Press E near a free companion of your species. Both take a meal, then meet again. Your companion may digest, visit a cleaner, or move away from danger. E ends the association. |
| Silver sprat, peach fairy basslet, purple anthias | Take plankton near two schoolmates, let a hungry neighbor feed, then rejoin the group. If the school moves on, follow it and try another patch. |
| Yellowtail fusilier | Join a moving school, make a feeding excursion, then return. Nearby hungry schoolmates can notice your plankton patch. |
| Yellow goatfish | Use E near a sand-search marker to inspect with your barbels. Eat the discovery or leave it for a nearby forager. A food click handles inspection and eating together. Moving or E stops inspection. |
| Longhorn cowfish | Use E to blow sand and uncover a meal. Search and eat at two different sites. Nearby foragers can notice uncovered food. |
| Coral grouper, devil scorpionfish, painted frogfish, longsnout flathead | Choose a marked ambush position and press E. Become still, aim with the mouse, then click a small nearby fish for a short strike. Frogfish wave their lure while waiting. E or clicking open water leaves the position. If a fish escapes, choose a position and try again. |
| Orange coral goby | Choose a coral home with E, settle, make a feeding trip, and return. |
| Cuttlefish | Use E to become still against a sandy background, then try a mottled reef surface. Swim away to leave concealment. |

There is no need to hurry through the journal. A quiet patch may have no hungry visitor yet; exploring another patch or habitat is useful. Schoolmates and companions keep their own appetite and routines. The map helps find other habitats, food and familiar places.

F11 switches fullscreen. Escape pauses the reef for inspection. Guide narration starts only when you press Play; it can be stopped independently of the music. Reduced motion is available in Settings.

These activities simplify animal behavior for a calm exploration game. Renewal rates, strike windows, social spacing and rewards are game rules rather than biological measurements. Challenge and Tournament retain their 55-species roster; the two cleaner roles are additional Practice choices.
