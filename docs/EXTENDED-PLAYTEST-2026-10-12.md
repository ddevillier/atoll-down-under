## Observations for subsequent polish

- Rabbitfish repeat encounters can wait several dozen active seconds for digestion or an available partner. The UI reports real busy/care/pairing states and earned discovery is retained, but the wait or fresh-dive choice can interrupt a child's momentum.
- Artist's completed optional three-pattern adventure and its ordinary two-pattern goal use separate counters. Returning to the ordinary card can appear to reset progress even though the adventure record remains intact.
- Nest completion can briefly coexist with the previous silt-carrying toast. The completion card and state are correct; the old toast is transient.
- Family journal is reached through Field guide while underwater. A just-earned badge may be harder to find than necessary. Full-shelf new-adventure admission also takes several safe save/export clicks.
- Small frogfish/gramma can blend into Coral Cathedral's painted detail. Density samples reached10–21 living actors, with at most seven neighboring centers within65 pixels; these are geometric measurements, not original-game parity or proof that painted overlaps are always readable.

## Evidence limits and remaining shipping gate

This is an extended, finite automated/control-driven audit, with actual UI play distinguished from model doses, inventories and labeled negative fixtures. It is not 57 completed species journeys, every random seed, exhaustive creator permutations, a human family verdict on fun, physical audio listening, a memory-leak endurance test or a security certification. The five older expanded modules do not expose the seven newer spoken Help controls; their absent buttons are not five failed recordings. Concurrent multi-worker slowdown is not normal single-instance performance evidence, and no animation quality reduction was made.

Earlier harness selector, arrival-tolerance, collapsed-panel and action-timing failures remain in the raw evidence and are excluded from product findings only where diagnosed and followed by accepted real-control results. No failed attempt was silently rewritten as a pass.

The detailed artifacts above are **local, gitignored audit evidence**, not files available from a fresh GitHub clone. This tracked report preserves their locations, accepted scope and limitations. Historical September parked/status documents describe earlier milestones and should not override this current audit. Final commit/push, fresh hosted CI through Windows packaging, and the exact combined Windows runtime/build receipt must still be recorded before shipping completion is claimed.
