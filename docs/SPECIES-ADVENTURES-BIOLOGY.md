# Species adventures: biology and interpretation

10 October 2026. This is the identity and wording contract for the seven new optional stories and the guide corrections accompanying them. The existing [sprint plan](SPECIES-ADVENTURE-SPRINT-PLAN.md) and [20-report research index](species-adventure-research/README.md) retain the wider researched queue. This document cites only the evidence used by these features; it does not expand that research scope or certify packaged-build acceptance.

All routes, contact thresholds, interaction times, role buttons, reactions and completion criteria are authored game interpretations. A player's choice is not evidence that a fish understands a human task. The catalog contains a fictional mixture of geographic ranges. Residents remain autonomous observation subjects, not newly playable characters.

| Registry key / discovery ID | Creature and story | Supported behavior and source | Game interpretation and excluded claims |
|---|---|---|---|
| `housekeeper` / `coral-housekeeper` | s30, *Dascyllus aruanus* — **Coral Housekeeper** | In an experiment with *Pocillopora damicornis*, occupied corals retained less sediment; picking, blowing and water movement contributed. [Chase et al. 2020](https://researchonline.jcu.edu.au/63854/), [research 01](species-adventure-research/01-coral-homes.md). | The colony is explicitly named. Two pockets and approach-direction particle feedback teach physical clearing. Uncovered branch detail represents sediment removal, without instant coral healing, growth, altruistic diagnosis or simulated nighttime oxygen exchange. |
| `lookout` / `rabbitfish-lookout` | s8 *Siganus vulpinus*, s52 *Siganus doliatus* — **Your Turn to Look** | The Lizard Island field study included both taxa and observed coordinated feeding/lookout postures and frequent role alternation. [Brandl and Bellwood 2015](https://www.nature.com/articles/srep14556), [research 06](species-adventure-research/06-paired-browsers.md). | Explicit player and companion roles make coordination visible. This observational evidence does not prove deliberate repayment, moral fairness or a decoded fin language. Species-specific statistical outcomes must not be treated as identical across both fish. Ordinary individual companion identity does not simulate lifelong biological pair formation. |
| `walker` / `frogfish-walk` | s54 *Antennarius pictus* — **A Walk Along the Reef** | The exact-species aquarium account describes moving on the bottom using pectoral and pelvic fins like limbs. [Okinawa Churaumi Aquarium](https://churaumi.okinawa/tc/fishbook/1459836633/), [research 12](species-adventure-research/12-camouflage.md). | Planted fin contacts and body travel express walking. A small foothold graph, reach bounds and compressed gait are authored geometry, not measured biomechanics. The existing lure/strike remains a separate activity; walking neither damages nor consumes sponges. |
| `nest` / `gramma-nest` | s3 *Gramma loreto* — **A Little Room Under the Roof** | Males prepare algae-associated nests in sheltered holes and alter entrances/remove silt; the study found no mouthbrooding. [Asoh and Yoshikawa 1996](https://doi.org/10.2307/1446936). Belly-up orientation beneath ledges is described by [Aquarium La Rochelle](https://www.aquarium-larochelle.com/en/species-encyclopedia/royal-gramma/). [Research 03](species-adventure-research/03-night-ledges.md). | The optional activity explicitly selects a nesting-male role. Tuft transport, entrance placement and clearing are construction actions, without calories, automatically appearing mates or eggs. It does not claim to scrub individual eggs. This Atlantic species is not presented as native to Australia. |
| `crevice` / `triggerfish-crevice` | s24 *Balistoides conspicillum* — **The Just-Right Crevice** | Clown triggerfish wedge into reef crevices and use the smaller dorsal spine to lock the first spine upright, then release it. [Waikīkī Aquarium](https://www.waikikiaquarium.org/experience/animal-guide/fishes/triggerfishes/clown-triggerfish/), [research 09](species-adventure-research/09-parrot-armored.md). | Authored openings make body fit, spine contact and release visible. Clearance thresholds are play rules; proximity to a shelter does not establish a lock. No predator arrival or guarantee of universal invulnerability is part of the biological claim. |
| `shade` / `squirrelfish-shade` | s12 *Neoniphon sammara* — **Someone in the Shade** | Exact-species field observations found mostly solitary fish, occasional shared shelter, and sound types without a one-to-one behavior code. [PLOS ONE 2024](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0312191), [research 03](species-adventure-research/03-night-ledges.md). | Designed sound/ripple cues accompany posture and actual spacing. They are not field recordings, translated greetings, distress-to-friendship claims or echolocation. The defensive sonic ability remains separately acknowledged fiction. Visual cues convey the same choices in muted play. |
| `lantern` / `lantern-company` | s53 Anomalopidae catalog entry; explicit *Anomalops katoptron* case study — **Lantern Company** | Studies connect visible bioluminescence with schooling, turns and nearest-neighbor distance in this named species. [Gruber et al. 2019](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0219852), [Hellinger et al. 2021](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7979757/), [research 16](species-adventure-research/16-schools-light.md). | The broad family roster identity is preserved. The activity explicitly names its case study and uses dedicated presentation. Route geometry, organ control, perception ranges and response strength are interpretations, not a universal flashlight-fish language. The social signal is separate from the defensive flash. Neither continuous light from every fish nor rapid strobing is required to explain the evidence. |

`src/natural-history.js` retains each established `source`/`url` and adds `storySources` for these newly described behaviors. Each added source already appears in its research report. *Siganus doliatus* keeps its documented fine blue lines and diagonal head bands. The named *Anomalops* treatment is confined to its case-study presentation; it does not silently rename the family catalog or saved creatures.

The guide now describes the already shipped five stories accurately: Reef Clinic uses contact treatment and remembers individual clients; Disappearing Artist offers three disguises and actual observer responses; Sandcastle Engineer represents sand deposition and existing algae depletion/renewal; Whisker Detective reveals buried meals and lets a real neighbor eat food left behind; Dad’s Big Mouth represents male mouthbrooding, four representative young and cover seeking. These rules do not measure actual sand production, sensory chemistry, altruism, biological brooding duration or natural brood size. Cleaner care restores parasite damage only.

Resident limitations now reflect existing ground movement and committed meals. Sea stars and urchins crawl despite zero catalog free-swimming speed; crabs, snails and nudibranchs use bounded bottom routes. The seahorse observation has a simplified meal/contact animation at its holdfast without fluid suction, courtship or brood care. These corrections do not add a new resident adventure, establish exact identities for generic sprites, or assign stolen stinging cells to the sponge-eating nudibranch. The doublelined mackerel already has ordinary schooling, distinct from the explicit plankton feeding-school activity.

The yellowspot emperor remains *Gnathodentex aureolineatus* with its current daytime game activity and saved dives intact. Its guide explicitly distinguishes documented daytime aggregation from nocturnal bottom feeding. Changing its active-period data requires the separate gameplay/community and save-compatibility review specified by [research 07](species-adventure-research/07-sand-detectives.md); this copy correction does not make that change.

Registry checks in `tests/reef-story-data.test.mjs` cover all 12 exact keys and discovery IDs, uniqueness, playable species membership, every species/story compatibility pair, missing defaults, prototype-key rejection, independent returned lists, a later second-story default, complete brief reflections, case-study identity and source metadata. `storyKeysFor(id)` returns all matching keys; `storyKeyFor(id)` retains the first-match default; `storyCompatible(id, key)` accepts only a registered compatible key. Mechanics, save lifecycle, motion/contact proof and Windows acceptance are verified by the integration work and its own tests, not by these metadata tests.
