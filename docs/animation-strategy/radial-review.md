## Geometry and integration

The first strong per-path anemone contraction failed the mesh orientation check: neighboring narrow influence regions could fold over each other. A continuous contraction around the oral disc now carries the crown inward while local path curls supply the variation. The final sampled meshes retain positive orientation at the tested effort, feeding and retraction extremes.

Sea-star travel and meal directions are read from existing simulation state, with no new saved fields. Eight direction bins participate in its deformation cache key so changing direction cannot reuse the opposite pose. Wrapped headings reuse their prior entry. All three rigs use the detailed 20×16 mesh; their incremental native performance cost remains unmeasured.
