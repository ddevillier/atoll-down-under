# Seven additional optional species adventures

Seven adventures are integrated with the five existing expanded stories. All twelve remain optional Practice activities within the existing nine habitats, 57 playable creatures and seven residents. The seven additions have independent browser acceptance through actual controls. The verification results and Windows package contract are recorded below.

Begin from the species picker for a guided entry, or open Activity in an existing dive. The latter reads the actual available encounter before offering Begin. Hiding the controls leaves the adventure active. Return to free exploration releases its guidance and physical pose; Continue retains its durable checkpoint. E performs the active story action. Existing species activities, including frogfish lure/ambush and rabbitfish feeding reunions, remain separate activities and discoveries.

## Shared runtime and save contracts

[reef-stories.js](../src/reef-stories.js) dispatches the twelve modules; [reef-story-data.js](../src/reef-story-data.js) owns their stable keys, discovery IDs, names and brief reflections. `storyKeysFor(id)` returns all compatible keys, `storyKeyFor(id)` keeps the first-match default for older callers, and explicit selection is checked by `storyCompatible(id,key)`.

Input, player movement, NPC movement and meal receipts have separate hooks. A hook that declines an input lets ordinary steering handle it. A held physical pose owns movement while it is active; decorative swimming, banking or body offsets cannot detach the painting from its contact. Starting another story releases competing ambush, watch, escort, cleaning and feeding assists. Continuing the same active checkpoint preserves its legitimate meal or held pose. NPC meal receipts reach actor-aware lookout logic; they cannot accidentally advance the older player-only meal hooks. Renderers paint state and never award progress.

[adventure-admission.js](../src/adventure-admission.js) scans real actors, bonds, care, committed meals, night context and available food without changing a living dive. Initial population allocation may specialize existing slots before that population is installed; it adds no adult slots. Bounded initial invitations prevent incidental feeding/pairing from consuming the opportunity before the player approaches. They expire or release when movement ownership is handed off, the player chooses another activity, or the story stops. Begin, Retry, Continue and import do not repair an encounter by spawning adults, teleporting companions, resetting appetite or healing them. Missing or exhausted participants produce a readable Retry or preserve-save/Fresh adventure dive path. An original companion finishing its real bite receives waiting guidance rather than a misleading fresh-dive prompt.

The existing version-one simulation state and version-two portable `.atoll` format contain bounded `reefStories` records. Old saves without stories, or with only the original five slots, remain compatible. Unknown keys, contradictory phases, invalid role/species combinations and forged physical held poses are rejected. Bound actors are resolved by reef, ID, incarnation and species; a reused ID cannot inherit an original companion's work. Continue retains the checkpoint and lets the module suspend or recover honestly when its actors are missing or busy.

Pause, blur/hidden handling and offscreen visits stop active work rather than completing it unseen. Saves retain the user's pause intention: restore first holds the imported simulation safely, then the normal entry flow resumes an unpaused save or leaves an explicitly paused save waiting for Resume. Interrupted transient jobs are released without inventing durable work; for example, nest lining and carried material survive, but unfinished clearing must regain real contact. Persisted discoveries remain unique after reload and replay.

[adventure-safety.js](../src/adventure-safety.js) derives a bounded current-reef scene from the active story and exact participants. Predators remain present and move around the occupied local interaction; ordinary predation elsewhere remains available. Local need holds preserve current energy, health, digestion or rest values where declared; they do not replenish them. Outside those holds, ordinary physiology continues, and the simulation ticks the new NPC needs once. Stopping first releases the module's spine, fins or work pose, then releases safety and reservations and grants the normal brief exit grace. Paused imported encounters derive the same release protection. There is no reef-wide fear effect or permanent Practice shield created by an adventure.

The addition includes **fourteen offline OGG clips**: one optional help clip and one brief discovery reflection for each new adventure. [The narration catalog](../src/discovery-narration-catalog.json) stores their text, durations, hashes and provenance; [generation provenance](../assets/audio/narration/species-adventures-provenance.json) records the audio production. Existing narration entries remain available. Help text and visible instructions work without playback; discovery narration follows the recorded consequence. Muted audio, reduced motion and reduced flash preserve essential posture, contact, spacing and exposure cues. Automatic narration honors opt-out, volume, pause and interruption. The application uses packaged audio and needs no runtime speech API or credential. Technical decoding/transcription checks do not establish family listening preference or approval.
